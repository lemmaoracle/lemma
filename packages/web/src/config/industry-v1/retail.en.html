01 — Why LemmaWhat keeps the customer waiting is the call to head office.

You want group-wide benefits and member perks to actually work across sites. But every store that isn't the issuing one has to check with head office, so nothing can be settled at the counter — and those few minutes of waiting are what the customer actually experiences. Pushing the data out to every store only moves the problem: now each of them carries the storage and the breach exposure. What you need is a way to confirm without distributing anything.
