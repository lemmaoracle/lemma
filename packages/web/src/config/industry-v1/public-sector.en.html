01 — Why LemmaWhat slows a procedure down is checking the paperwork.

A resident collects a tax certificate at one counter and submits it at another, where someone confirms by eye that it is genuine. One document gets handled three times — issued, presented, checked. Digitizing it does not remove the loop, because anyone can produce a PDF: if the receiving side cannot check independently, it ends up asking for the original anyway. What is missing alongside digitization is a way to check.

handled three times.
