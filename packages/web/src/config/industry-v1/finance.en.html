01 — Why LemmaWhat slows a review down is the round-trip.

Credit decisions, KYC, sanctions screening — the judgment itself takes minutes. The time goes into confirmation: querying the issuer, waiting for the answer, requesting the original. Digitizing the paperwork does not remove that loop, because anyone can produce a PDF. The wait ends only when the receiving side can check for itself.

The review isn't slow.
It's waiting on the issuer.
