01 — Why LemmaWhat delays staffing is checking the paperwork.

Meeting a staffing standard takes more than having a qualified person present: you have to show on paper that the licence is valid, the training was completed, and the person is fit to work. Those documents get copied, checked against originals and filed again at every hire and every renewal. The faster people rotate through a site, the more that manual checking becomes the bottleneck.

It isn't the staff running short.
It's the checking.

Verify authenticity without holding personal data

Diagnoses and test values never reach us. Tampering is checked against a hash alone, so the exposure you carry stays small.

POST /v1/documents
// Only the hash is sent — never the contents { "integrityHash": "sha256:…", "issuerId": "nurse-assoc" } // What comes back { "docHash": "0x68d4…9f01" }

On health information: we do not handle diagnoses or test values themselves. Proof is attached to the record that a check took place, or to the fitness decision, and confirmed on presentation.
