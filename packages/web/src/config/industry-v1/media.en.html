01 — Why LemmaLooking at it is no longer enough to tell.

Deepfakes and generative models can now produce a photo, a paragraph or a video that nobody can tell from the real thing by eye. What you have to protect is your own output. Finding fakes and taking them down is a race you do not win; being able to point at the genuine one first is both faster and more certain.
