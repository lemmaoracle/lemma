01 — Why LemmaWhat slows review down is collecting, eyeballing and tracking expiry.

Supplier qualification is three jobs — collect the certificate, check it by eye, track when it expires. All three are manual and all three recur. With several hundred suppliers, a dedicated team still cannot keep up. And whether a submitted certificate is genuine is, in the end, never established: what gets confirmed is that it arrived, not that it is valid.

What you want isn't the document.
It's the fact that it's valid.
