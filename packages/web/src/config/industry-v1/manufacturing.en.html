01 — Why LemmaWhat delays a shipment is the checking that happens after you hand it over.

Inspection records and measurements are managed properly inside the plant. The problem starts downstream: the moment they reach a customer or a regulator, the receiving side holds no grounds for calling them genuine. So originals get requested, witness inspections are scheduled, and the same content is explained again and again. What is in doubt is not the quality of the record — it is whether there is any way to check it.

The quality isn't in doubt.
There's just no way to show it.
