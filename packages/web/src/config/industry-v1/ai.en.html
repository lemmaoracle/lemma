01 — Why LemmaAs AI adoption spreads, so does the demand to account for it

More work runs through AI every quarter, and so do the occasions where someone asks why a particular decision came out that way. What gets tested is less the substance of the decision than whether the record of it is genuine. Narrowing permissions is not enough on its own, because the routes that deceive an agent operate inside its legitimate authority. What is missing is a layer that establishes whose request this is before anything executes.

What gets tested isn't how smart the AI is.
It's where the record came from.
