Only the issuing side pays

The cost does not move however many parties verify, or how often. They do not need an account either.

We never hold your raw data

Lemma receives only a hash; the record itself stays with you. Handing over a proof does not hand over the contents.

The issuer is recorded in the proof

Whoever receives it can confirm that you issued it (how your organization name appears is set during onboarding). No one else can issue in your name.
