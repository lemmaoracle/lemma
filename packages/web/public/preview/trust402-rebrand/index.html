why proof matters

Copies of your work are everywhere.

Scraped versions and AI-made imitations sit right next to your original. Without proof, an agent can't tell which one is really yours — so it might pay someone else, or trust a fake.

A copy can look identical. Only yours can prove it.

The technical way to say it: Signed is the only real.

A signature proving you're the publisher — you can reveal only the parts you want. (BBS+ over BLS12-381)
not changed

So an agent always knows it's citing the exact version you meant.

questions students ask

Good things to check.

Are Pay and Sell separate plans?

No — it's one set of plans. Explorer, Pro, and Institutional ID each cover both Pay and Sell, with a single monthly allowance shared across the two. Checking proofs is always free on either side.

I've never done this — where do I start?

In the free sandbox. Fill one short form on a test network, watch the whole flow happen, and pay nothing. When it clicks, going live is the same form.

Do I need to know anything about crypto or blockchain?

You receive USDC — a digital dollar — to your own wallet, so you set one up once (a 2-minute step if you don't have one). We handle all the proof and payment plumbing behind the scenes.

How do I actually get paid?

In USDC, a digital dollar, straight to your own wallet — self-custody, we never hold your funds. When an agent buys your work, the payment settles automatically and shows up in your wallet, no invoicing.

Do I have to give away my data to prove it's mine?

No. Agents check a proof, not the raw file. Your data stays with you until someone actually pays for it.

Can I still use or sell my work somewhere else?

Yes. It's yours. Selling through Lemma doesn't lock you in or take exclusive rights.

I'm a student — where do I start, and is there a cost?

Start free in the sandbox to try it with no real money. When you're ready to go live, publish free under your school's institutional plan — still credited to you by name.

Is this a marketplace? Does Lemma resell my data?

No. You sell; Lemma is just the tool that makes it possible. Discovery happens in the open x402 Bazaar.
